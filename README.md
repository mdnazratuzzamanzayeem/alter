## 📌 Overview

**Alter** is a minimalist, reader-centric digital publication and personal journal designed to foster deep, uninterrupted engagement with critical social, educational, and policy issues. Built as a fast, clean static website hosted on GitHub Pages, **Alter** cuts through digital noise to deliver well-researched, long-form commentary on societal dilemmas and systemic challenges.

Whether dissecting systemic flaws in national examination systems or tackling deeply ingrained societal taboos, Alter serves as an archive of critical dispatches aimed at educating, challenging, and inspiring meaningful conversation.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

- **📖 Distraction-Free Typography & UI:** Minimalist aesthetic designed specifically for long-form readability, prioritizing typography, clean spacing, and zero intrusive ads or popups.
- **⚡ Blazing Fast Performance:** Lightweight static architecture delivering near-instant page load times and minimal resource consumption.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Optimized seamlessly across mobile devices, tablets, and desktop displays.
- **🗂️ Categorized Articles & Dispatches:** Organized by core themes including *Society & Health*, *Education*, *Technology & Policy*, and broad cultural commentary.
- **🏛️ Built-in Archiving & Navigation:** Includes easy access to article metadata (reading time estimates, publication dates, and category tags).


---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend:** HTML5, Modern CSS3 (Flexbox/Grid layout, custom variables, custom typography via Google Fonts)
- **Deployment & Hosting:** [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/)
- **Icons & Assets:** Optimized lightweight web graphics & SVG icon sets
